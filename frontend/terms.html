What the service is

EchoSeal embeds an inaudible identifier into speech generated by organisations that have registered with us, and lets anyone check a recording for that identifier. It is provided for demonstration and evaluation.

What a result means, and what it does not

A verified result means a watermark was detected and matched an entry in our demo registry. It does not certify the content of the call, the truthfulness of anything said, or that any request made during the call is legitimate.

An unverified result means no watermark was found. This is the expected result for almost all audio, because almost no organisation has registered. It is not evidence that a call is fraudulent, and it must not be presented to anyone as such.

Detection is imperfect. Poor recordings can fail to verify audio that genuinely carries a mark. The identifier is carried in sixteen bits with no secret key, and the underlying watermarking model is open source, so a determined attacker who knows how this system works could embed a registered identifier into their own audio. Do not rely on EchoSeal as a sole security control.

Do not rely on this for financial decisions

Never approve a payment, share a one time password, or grant access to a device based on a result from this tool. If a call asks for any of those, end it and call back on a number you obtained independently.
